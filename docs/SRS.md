# Purpose and System Overview
This document is the Software Requirements Specification for SitNeat. It explains what the system must do and how it will be tested. The PRD explains the product goals and this document turns those goals into clear requirements. The TDD will describe how the system is built. SitNeat is a single restaurant web application. The frontend uses React.js with TypeScript. The backend uses Python with FastAPI. PostgreSQL stores the data. The backend exposes REST APIs and is responsible for validating requests and enforcing permissions.

# Terms and User Roles
A seat is a bookable table or seating unit with a unique number and capacity. A reservation is a booking for one seat, one party, and one time period. RBAC means role based access control where a role determines a user's general capabilities. A security scope is a named permission required by an API operation. An ownership check makes sure a customer accesses their own booking. The MVP is the smallest complete version required for this course project.

There are three roles. A customer can register, log in, view profile, check availability, and create view edit or cancel their own eligible bookings. A customer cannot see other customers' bookings or use staff and manager tools. A staff member can view today's operational bookings, see service related food and allergy details, update permitted statuses, and cancel a confirmed booking on a guest's behalf. A staff member cannot manage accounts, roles, seats, or settings. A manager can view and filter all bookings, manage seats and staff, change settings, update permitted statuses, and view summary counts. A manager cannot create or promote manager accounts through the app.

Public registration must always create a customer. The backend must not trust a role or customer ID sent by the browser. Staff accounts are managed by a manager. Manager setup is handled outside the normal staff management screen.

# Authentication Requirements
FR-AUTH-001 says the system shall let visitors register with full name, email, and password. FR-AUTH-002 says public registration shall create a customer account only and a submitted role value shall not grant extra access. FR-AUTH-003 says the system shall let active users log in with email and password. FR-AUTH-004 says the system shall reject invalid credentials, inactive accounts, and missing invalid or expired tokens. FR-AUTH-005 says passwords shall be stored as secure hashes and not plaintext. FR-AUTH-006 says an authenticated user shall be able to view their own profile and the frontend shall provide logout. FR-AUTH-007 says only a manager shall create activate or deactivate staff accounts through manager endpoints.

# Seat Requirements
FR-SEAT-001 says a manager shall create seats with a unique seat number, capacity, optional description, and active flag. FR-SEAT-002 says a manager shall update a seat's number, capacity, description, or active flag. FR-SEAT-003 says seat capacity shall be positive and within the agreed limit of one to twenty. FR-SEAT-004 says inactive seats shall not be offered for new bookings or selected during a booking change. FR-SEAT-005 says the seat list shall show active seats and managers may request inactive seats for administration. FR-SEAT-006 says availability search shall accept a date, start time, and party size and apply restaurant hours and booking rules. FR-SEAT-007 says availability results shall include seat ID, seat number, capacity, description, and availability.

# Reservation Requirements
FR-BOOK-001 says a customer shall create a reservation for one active seat and a valid time slot. FR-BOOK-002 says the backend shall calculate the reservation end time using the configured duration. FR-BOOK-003 says the system shall recheck capacity, availability, operating hours, and booking rules when creating or changing a reservation. FR-BOOK-004 says a reservation saved successfully shall be confirmed automatically. FR-BOOK-005 says the system shall prevent overlapping active reservations for the same seat including concurrent requests. FR-BOOK-006 says customers shall list and view only their own reservations and history. FR-BOOK-007 says customers shall change their own confirmed reservations only before the configured cutoff. FR-BOOK-008 says customers shall cancel their own confirmed reservations only before the configured cutoff. FR-BOOK-009 says a change shall be rejected if the new seat or time is unavailable or a booking rule fails. FR-BOOK-010 says cancelling a confirmed reservation shall release the seat for that time.

Food and Allergy Requirements
FR-FOOD-001 says each reservation shall require vegetarian or non-vegetarian as food preference. FR-FOOD-002 says each reservation shall require no known allergies or allergies specified as a separate declaration. FR-FOOD-003 says allergy details shall be required and length limited when allergies specified is selected. FR-FOOD-004 says food preference and allergy declaration shall be stored independently. FR-FOOD-005 says allergy details shall be visible only to the customer who owns the booking, authorized staff, and managers.

# Staff Requirements
FR-STAFF-001 says staff shall see confirmed and seated reservations for the current restaurant day. FR-STAFF-002 says staff shall change confirmed to seated and seated to completed when the transition rules allow it. FR-STAFF-003 says staff shall be able to cancel a confirmed booking on a guest's behalf. FR-STAFF-004 says staff shall not manage accounts, roles, seats, or restaurant settings.

# Manager Requirements
FR-MGR-001 says a manager shall view and filter all reservations by date and status. FR-MGR-002 says a manager shall create, update, activate, and deactivate seats. FR-MGR-003 says a manager shall create and update staff accounts and activate or deactivate staff. FR-MGR-004 says a manager shall configure opening hours, reservation duration, slot interval, minimum lead time, advance booking window, and customer change cutoff. FR-MGR-005 says a manager should view total reservations and counts by status for a chosen date range. FR-MGR-006 says staff management endpoints shall not create, promote, or demote manager accounts.

# Role and Security Requirements
FR-ROLE-001 says every protected API operation shall check permissions on the backend. FR-ROLE-002 says the backend shall check reservation ownership independently of frontend route guards. FR-ROLE-003 says customers shall not set customer ID, role, status, end time, or other server controlled reservation fields. FR-ROLE-004 says a customer accessing another customer's reservation shall receive a not found response without details about that booking. FR-ROLE-005 says users shall not grant themselves permissions or change their own role.

# Business Rules and Status Flow
BR-01 says the MVP supports one restaurant and one restaurant time zone. BR-02 says each reservation uses one seat and party size must be at least one and no greater than capacity. BR-03 says end time equals start time plus the configured reservation duration and the customer does not submit end time. BR-04 says a seat must be active and free for the full reservation period. BR-05 says confirmed and seated reservations block overlapping bookings for the same seat. BR-06 says successful new bookings are confirmed immediately and there is no pending status. BR-07 says the allowed flow is confirmed to seated to completed and confirmed may instead become cancelled. BR-08 says completed and cancelled are final statuses and seated bookings cannot be cancelled in the MVP. BR-09 says customer changes and cancellations must be before the cutoff and staff or managers may cancel confirmed bookings on a guest's behalf. BR-10 says allergy information is customer declared and does not mean SitNeat guarantees allergen free food.

 # REST API Requirements
All paths start with slash api slash v1. JSON is used for request and response bodies. Protected routes need a valid access token and the required scope. The backend must also check the user's role and access to the requested record. There is a public endpoint to register a customer account and it returns 201. There is a public endpoint to log in and it returns 200. There is an authenticated endpoint to read the current profile and it returns 200. There is an endpoint to list seats with scope seats read and it returns 200. There is an endpoint to search availability with scope seats read and it returns 200. There is an endpoint to create a seat with scope seats manage and it returns 201. There is an endpoint to update a seat with scope seats manage and it returns 200. There is an endpoint to read restaurant settings with scope settings read and it returns 200. There is an endpoint to update settings with scope settings manage and it returns 200. There is an endpoint to create a reservation with scope reservations create own and it returns 201. There is an endpoint to list own reservations with scope reservations read own and it returns 200. There is an endpoint to read one reservation with role or scope plus ownership and it returns 200. There is an endpoint to edit an eligible own booking with scope reservations update own and it returns 200. There is an endpoint to cancel an eligible confirmed booking with own or operational permission and it returns 200. There is an endpoint to update allowed status with scope reservations update operational and it returns 200. There is an endpoint to list today's operational bookings with scope reservations read operational and it returns 200. There is an endpoint to list and filter all bookings with scope reservations read all and it returns 200. There is an endpoint to create a staff account with scope staff manage and it returns 201. There is an endpoint to list staff accounts with scope staff manage and it returns 200. There is an endpoint to update activate or deactivate staff with scope staff manage and it returns 200. There is an endpoint to read date range counts with scope reports read and it returns 200. There is a public health check endpoint that returns 200.

# Data Requirements
The user entity has required fields including ID, full name, normalized email, password hash, role, active flag, and timestamps. Email is unique. Role is customer, staff, or manager. Password is never stored as plain text. The seat entity has required fields including ID, seat number, capacity, optional description, active flag, and timestamps. Seat number is unique. Capacity is one to twenty. Inactive seats cannot be newly booked. The reservation entity has required fields including ID, customer ID, seat ID, start and end timestamps, party size, food preference, allergy declaration and details, status, and timestamps. References must exist. End follows start. Party fits. Status is one of four allowed values. The restaurant settings entity has opening hours, duration, slot interval, minimum lead time, advance booking window, change cutoff, and time zone. Values are validated and new settings apply to later bookings. Use primary keys, foreign keys, unique constraints, and validation check constraints. Add indexes for common searches by customer, seat, start time, and status. Use a database transaction and a concurrency safe constraint or locking strategy to prevent double booking. Store timestamps consistently and use the restaurant time zone for opening hours and daily staff lists.

# Security Requirements
SEC-01 says hash passwords using a maintained algorithm such as Argon2id or bcrypt. SEC-02 says sign access tokens, set an expiry, and validate each protected request. SEC-03 says keep signing secrets and database credentials out of source control. SEC-04 says enforce scopes and roles on the backend and frontend guards are not a security control. SEC-05 says check reservation ownership and never trust customer IDs supplied by the browser. SEC-06 says only a manager may manage staff accounts or restaurant settings. SEC-07 says validate request data and return safe error messages. SEC-08 says never log passwords, tokens, database secrets, or full allergy details. SEC-09 says allow only the approved frontend origin through CORS in deployment. SEC-10 says use HTTPS in deployment and cookie based authentication needs CSRF protection and bearer token storage must be chosen carefully. SEC-11 says limit allergy data to the customer, authorized staff, and managers. SEC-12 says record important manager actions without storing sensitive details in audit logs.

# Non Functional Requirements
NFR-01 says forms have clear labels and helpful errors and pages show loading, empty, success, and failure states. NFR-02 says core navigation and forms support keyboard use and accessible labels. NFR-03 says for the course demo dataset, normal read requests should usually complete within two seconds on the development setup excluding slow network conditions. NFR-04 says the database prevents overlapping active bookings for the same seat including concurrent requests. NFR-05 says separate API routes, schemas, business logic, and database models into clear modules. NFR-06 says support recent versions of major desktop and mobile browsers. NFR-07 says collect only necessary information, restrict allergy details, and define a retention approach. NFR-08 says the repository includes setup instructions, environment example, API docs, PRD, SRS, and TDD. NFR-09 says automated tests cover booking rules, authentication, roles, ownership, and status transitions. NFR-10 says API URL, database connection, signing secret, and restaurant settings can be configured without code changes.

# Error Handling
Errors should use a consistent JSON structure with a stable code and a short message. For example, if a seat is booked by someone else before a customer submits, return HTTP 409 with code SEAT UNAVAILABLE. Field errors should explain what needs to be fixed. Unexpected errors should be logged with a request ID but stack traces, credentials, and database details must not be sent to the browser.

# Main Use Cases
UC-01 is register. A visitor enters name, email, and password. The system validates the details and creates a customer account. A duplicate email produces a clear error. UC-02 is log in. A user enters credentials. The system checks the password and account status then returns a token and safe profile details. Invalid credentials produce a generic error. UC-03 is check availability. A user chooses date, start time, and party size. SitNeat checks hours, booking limits, seat capacity, and existing bookings then returns suitable seats. UC-04 is book a seat. A customer selects a seat and enters food and allergy details. The system validates again and saves a confirmed booking or returns a conflict if the seat is no longer available. UC-05 is change or cancel. A customer changes or cancels their own confirmed booking before the cutoff. The system checks ownership, cutoff, capacity, and availability. UC-06 is staff operations. Staff view today's bookings, check permitted details, update status, or cancel a confirmed booking on a guest's behalf. UC-07 is manager operations. A manager manages seats and staff, changes settings, filters bookings, and views summary counts.

# Acceptance and Test Plan
T-01 says register with role set to manager and the account is created as customer only. T-02 says log in with wrong password or inactive account and access is denied safely. T-03 says call protected API without a valid token and the API returns 401. T-04 says customer A requests customer B's booking and the API returns 404 and does not reveal booking details. T-05 says customer tries to manage staff or seats and the API denies the request. T-06 says party size is larger than seat capacity and the request is rejected. T-07 says customer books an inactive seat and the request is rejected. T-08 says two overlapping requests try to book the same seat and at most one succeeds and the other receives a conflict. T-09 says allergies specified but details are blank and the request is rejected with a field error. T-10 says customer tries to edit or cancel after cutoff and the request is rejected. T-11 says attempt confirmed to completed directly and the request is rejected. T-12 says staff tries to change restaurant settings and the API denies the request. T-13 says manager changes opening hours or duration and valid settings are saved and applied to future bookings. T-14 says an unexpected server error occurs and the response does not reveal stack trace or secrets. Use backend unit tests, API integration tests, authorization tests for all three roles, PostgreSQL tests for booking conflicts, and frontend tests for forms and error states. Add an end to end booking test if time allows.
